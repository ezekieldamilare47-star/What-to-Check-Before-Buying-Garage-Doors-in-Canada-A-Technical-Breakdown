# What to Check Before Buying Garage Doors in Canada: A Technical Breakdown

## Summary

Buying a garage door or configuring one online shouldn't mean guessing at what the numbers mean. This breakdown covers the specific, checkable factors that matter.

## Technical Checklist

### 1. Context
- A garage door is the largest moving part of a home and often the largest single element of its front elevation. It affects heat loss in winter, noise, security and curb appeal every day, which makes it one of the few exterior upgrades that is both functional and highly visible. In a Canadian climate, insulation and sealing matter as much as appearance.

### 2. Background and Construction
- [Ryterna](https://www.ryterna.com/sectional-garage-doors/) is a manufacturer established in 1993 that makes overhead sectional, hinged and side sliding doors for garages and industry, as well as house entrance doors. Its doors are made to order, with customized design and dimensions.
- Ryterna doors are manufactured in Lithuania by a company established in 1993. They use insulated steel sandwich-panel construction with polyurethane foam cores, with 0.5 mm steel skins on the embossed and smooth panels and 0.7 mm skins on the Slick design panels, depending on the product selected. Guiding tracks are made from 1.5 mm or 2 mm steel depending on the track system, and tracks can be customized to the garage and roof pitch.

### 3. Detail
- Insulated panel options of 40 mm and 60 mm are available on applicable products, with documented thermal specifications. Calculated panel insulation equivalents are approximately R-12 and R-16 respectively, based on the supplied technical U-values. These are panel values, not whole-door ratings, and perimeter and inter-panel sealing, plus multiple sealing and track options, complete the system.

### 4. Options
- Doors are made to order in custom dimensions up to 8 m wide, with Okoume hardwood panels, stainless steel appliques and windows, acrylic double-glazed windows in clear, matt, frosted or tinted finishes, full-view panels in any RAL colour or Golden Oak, and pass doors up to 1.2 m wide with a low threshold. For a carriage house garage door look, the Georgian style and wood-look finishes in the configurator are a natural starting point, while Slim-Line and full-view designs suit a modern garage door.

### 5. Coverage and Standards
- Published coverage includes 10 years on panels against rust-through or foam delamination and five years on specified moving parts, subject to the catalogue's model and usage conditions. The products are CE-marked, with documented European standards and a registered environmental product declaration.

### 6. Availability and Support
- Ryterna doors are sold across Canada through Garage Door Fix, which is listed on Ryterna's contact page as a Canadian distribution partner. Doors can be shipped anywhere in Canada, and Garage Door Fix installs in Alberta.
